Franchise under ₹30 lakh in Maharashtra: how to choose one that pays

Searching for a profitable franchise is easy; working out which one will actually pay you back is harder. Here are the seven things to compare before you sign, how the main franchise types differ, and how a ₹26 lakh EV fleet franchise works, with the numbers and their limits.

Seven things to compare before you invest

Put every franchise you're considering through the same questions. The answers matter more than the brand name or the headline return.

  1. The real total investment

    Add the franchise fee, fit-out or assets, deposits, launch costs and a few months of running costs. Ask the brand: what is the full amount before my first rupee of income, and which parts are refundable?

  2. Who brings the customers

    Some franchises rely on walk-in footfall at your location; others bring demand to you. Ask: where will my customers come from in the first 90 days, and what does the brand do to find them?

  3. Fixed monthly costs

    Rent, salaries, royalties and lease charges are paid whether business is good or slow. Ask: what are my fixed costs each month, and at what level of sales do I break even?

  4. How and when you are paid

    Cash shops collect daily; platform businesses settle on a cycle. Ask: who collects the money, how often is my share paid, and can I see every transaction?

  5. Who runs it day to day

    Owner-operated models need your time; managed models need less of it but share more revenue. Ask: how many hours a week will this take me, and what does the brand do for me?

  6. What you own at the end

    Some franchises leave you with a lease and fittings; others leave you with assets that keep working. Ask: what do I own, and what happens if I want to exit?

  7. Territory and contract terms

    Check exclusivity, performance targets, renewal and exit clauses. Ask: is my area protected, what targets must I hit, and what happens if I miss them?

Questions people ask

Is an EV franchise profitable?

It can be, but it depends on deployment. At full deployment and ₹6,600 per scooter per month, a 50-scooter YuGO FOFO fleet shows about ₹1.53 lakh a month and a payback of about 17 months. Break-even is around 54% deployment. These figures are illustrative, not guaranteed.

Can I get a franchise under ₹30 lakh in Pune or PCMC?

Yes. The YuGO FOFO model starts at ₹26 lakh for 50 scooters, plus a refundable battery deposit of about ₹2.93 lakh. Territories across Maharashtra are subject to availability.

Do I need experience in EVs or delivery?

No EV experience is required. YuGO provides launch support, systems and rider leads. FOFO suits people who can run a small hub and team; if you'd rather not run operations, the FOCO model has YuGO run the fleet for you.

How is my money collected and paid?

Riders pay through YuGO. Payments are tagged to each of your scooters and settled to you every 15 days on cleared collections, after battery, Fleet Support and spare-parts deductions, with every transaction visible in your portal.
