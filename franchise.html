A fleet business built around utilisation

Own productive EV assets and share in the recurring economics of last-mile delivery. This is more than buying scooters: the franchise bundles the asset with a local operating system for rider acquisition, deployment, energy, service and collections.

  • Demand

    Quick-commerce and last-mile delivery riders need reliable, affordable vehicles every day.

  • Energy

    Battery swapping cuts charging downtime and keeps riders earning, so scooters stay on the road.

  • Technology

    Vehicle visibility, collections and controls are built into the model through the YuGO app, portal and IoT.

FOFO or FOCO: two ways to own the asset

In both models you own the scooters and YuGO runs the platform, battery network, rider demand and settlement. Pick based on how much day-to-day running you want to do.

Franchise FAQs

How much do I need to invest?

FOFO starts at ₹26 lakh for 50 scooters (fleet investment plus franchise fee), plus a refundable battery deposit of about ₹2.93 lakh. FOCO starts at ₹52 lakh for 100 scooters (deposit about ₹5.85 lakh) or ₹77 lakh for 160 scooters (deposit about ₹9.36 lakh).

What is the difference between FOFO and FOCO?

In both you own the scooters. In FOFO you run the local hub, staff and daily operations and keep the operating profit after YuGO's charges. In FOCO YuGO runs everything day to day; you provide space and utilities and receive 65% of gross rider revenue.

Do I have to buy batteries?

No. Batteries are leased from YuGO at ₹1,500 per battery per month (2,000 km included, then ₹1.25 per km), with a refundable deposit of ₹4,500 per battery. Plan for about 1.3 batteries per scooter.

Where do the riders come from?

YuGO's demand engine and campaigns generate rider leads for your fleet. In FOFO you convert and retain them; in FOCO YuGO does this for you.

How and when am I paid?

Rider payments are collected by YuGO, tagged to each of your scooters, and settled to you every 15 days against cleared collections, after battery, Fleet Support and spare-parts deductions. Everything is visible in your portal.

Is the payback guaranteed?

No. The payback periods (about 17 months for FOFO 50, 21–23 months for FOCO) are illustrative and assume full deployment at ₹6,600 per scooter per month. They exclude financing, tax, ramp-up and the refundable deposit. Real results depend on deployment, rider behaviour, collections and local costs.

Will I get an exclusive territory?

You get a defined micro-market. Exclusivity is conditional on meeting performance standards, and strong performers can get first right to expand into neighbouring areas, subject to the final agreement.
