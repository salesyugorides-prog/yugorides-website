How it works

From sign-up to your first delivery in four steps.

  1. Sign up in the app

    Register in the YuGO Rides app. KYC is completed automatically in the app, then choose your plan.

  2. Collect your scooter

    Pick up your YuGO scooter, check it over with our team and start riding.

  3. Swap your battery

    When the battery runs low, swap it for a charged one at a swap station and ride on.

  4. Keep riding, renew or return

    Continue on your plan, renew, or return the scooter as per your rental terms.

How battery swapping works

Instead of plugging in and waiting, you exchange the scooter's low battery for a fully charged one.

  • Find a stationUse the YuGO Rides app to find a swap station near you.
  • Swap the batteryTake out the low battery and put in a charged one. It typically takes about 2 minutes.
  • Ride onA charged battery is advertised at around 70–75 km per swap. Swaps are unlimited on both plans.

FAQs

Which plan should I choose?

If you ride less than 650 km a week, the Light Rider Plan is ₹1,550 per week with up to 650 km included and ₹2 per km after that. If you ride 1,000 km or more a week, the Heavy Rider Plan is ₹69 per day plus ₹1.50 per km, with a minimum of 1,000 km per week. Call us if you're unsure and we'll help you pick.

Are battery swaps really unlimited?

Yes. Both current plans include unlimited battery swaps.

How long does a battery swap take?

A swap typically takes about 2 minutes. It can take longer if there's a queue at the station.

How far can I ride on one battery?

Our advertised range is around 70–75 km per swap. Real-world range depends on load, speed, traffic and road conditions.

Where do you operate?

We serve delivery riders in Pune and Pimpri-Chinchwad (PCMC).

How do I sign up?

Download the YuGO Rides app and sign up. KYC is completed automatically in the app. If you need help, call us.

Who looks after maintenance?

YuGO takes care of maintenance for your rented scooter. If something isn't working right, call us on 80805 13935 or 91753 24002.
